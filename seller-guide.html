💗 How to Add a Product

It's super easy. No law degree required. 👩‍⚖️

1

📸 Add Photos

Upload clear pictures of your product. Good lighting is your best friend.

More sparkle, more attention! ✨
2

📝 Add Details

Enter the product name, description, category and condition.

Tell the truth, bestie. 💋
3

💰 Set Price

Choose a reasonable starting price. Don't price it like it's the Crown Jewels.

Know your worth! 👑
4

📅 Set Duration

Select how long your auction will run, such as 1, 3 or 7 days.

Time is literally money. ⏰
5

🚀 Publish

Click the publish button and let the bidding chaos begin!

Go make us proud, seller! 💗

💅 How to Change Your Price

Changed your mind? Totally understandable.

1

📋 Open Listings

Go to your profile and open the My Listings section.

2

✏️ Click Edit

Find the product you want to update and select Edit.

3

💸 Update Details

Change the starting price, description, photos or duration.

4

💾 Save Changes

Click Save and check that your updated listing looks perfect.

⚠️ Important: Price changes may be restricted once bidding has started.
