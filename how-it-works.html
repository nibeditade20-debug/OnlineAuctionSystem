BEFORE YOU BID

Auction Rules

01

Registration is required. You must have an AuctionAura account before placing a bid.

02

Every bid is binding. Once submitted, a bid cannot be withdrawn or cancelled.

03

Bid higher than the current bid. Your new bid must exceed the displayed current highest bid.

04

Watch the auction timer. Bids must be submitted before the auction closes.

05

The highest bidder wins. The user with the highest valid bid when the auction ends is declared the winner.

06

Bid responsibly. Only place bids that you are prepared and able to honour.
